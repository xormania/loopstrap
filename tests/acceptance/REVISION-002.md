# Acceptance basis revision 002

The owner clarified the runtime ontology after the original Treatment claims
were frozen: a Role is a responsibility, while a Role-Treatment is its exact
harness-specific realization. Harness and model provider are independent, and
the runtime must not trust a harness echo as proof of effective configuration.

TREAT-01 through TREAT-04 and TREAT-07 are therefore strengthened and their
witness names updated. The revised basis binds Role, harness, provider/model
route, native reasoning and orchestration control, wrapper, configuration, and
launch attestation that separates requested, sent, and observed state. No
acceptance obligation was removed.
