## 0 · The containment principle *(P17 — the topology's reason, on the record)*

- **Repos are decision-containment cells.** Each member repo is the blast radius of one Conductor: the conventions, judgments, and half-decisions a loop makes are trapped inside its cell. Capabilities cross cell walls only as **ratified interfaces cited at pinned revs** — never as shared interiors. This is xor's founding rationale for the repo topology; the dev lane mirrors the runtime law (projects never push; outbound only through a licensed, gated process — the same law, both lanes).
- **The sweep is the sole export path.** A decision leaves a cell and becomes family law only by xor's dispositional act at the sweep (§3.5) — the customs office at the cell wall. Interior decisions never escalate on their own.
- **Parallelism is licensed by containment.** Conductors in separate cells cannot contaminate each other by construction; concurrency is the design default, gated per member by §1's conditions — never by a fleet queue.
- **The cell principle, recursive form (L17).** The unit of parallel-safe work at ANY scale — member, segment, lane, unit, split-child — is a contract-bounded black box: its seam to siblings fully covered by ratified clauses at pinned hashes, its interior invisible. Work parallelizes exactly where the seam is contract-covered; an under-specified seam serializes or halts (the footing license never covers it). Recursion is the same law downward: a decomposition is valid when children preserve the parent's outer seam while partitioning its interior (L9/L10's scope-narrowing invariant). Inside a unit, the Intended-API surface (L8) is the same seam between the blind test-writer and the implementer.
- **The wall-vs-interior test governs family rulings.** Uniform rulings are justified only where **wall construction** demands them — deterministic judges, join keys, exclusion mechanics, the failure vocabulary at interfaces. A member's **interior** choices stay contained; "prevent divergence" is never, by itself, grounds for a family ruling on interiors.
- **The interface-cell exception** was a donor-family construct (a shared definitions crate); with a single member it has no referent and is retired. It re-enters only if xor adds members whose interiors are shared interfaces.

## 1 · The campaign

A **campaign** is the managed unit: one Conductor, one member, one kickoff + curated backlog, under one rulings snapshot, run unattended to exhaustion or halt. ID: `<member>-c<N>`.

**Concurrency is the design default (§0); serial is a bootstrap condition** with named exit gates `[drafting decision — confirm: gate list]`:

1. *(retired with the interface-cell exception — single member)*;
2. the FR queue's **wall** items are ruled (toolchain/edition as judge-determinism walls, lockfile, join-key travel);
3. provisioning isolates shared machine state per §11 (Conductors never write shared homes at run time; trust pre-recorded fleet-wide; contention-free credential paths);
4. per candidate member: its basis exists (spec, plus experience spec where surfaced) and its known conflicts are ruled.

When the gates clear, members arm concurrently on their own per-cell gates — the board carries gates, never a queue. **Within a member, parallelism rides lanes (L16):** at Prepare the plan pipeline emits lane assignments — backlog units partitioned into lanes by **clause-seam disjointness — the criterion (L17)**; disjoint file footprints are the verified *consequence*, checked at plan review — clause-disjoint lanes with overlapping expected footprints are an **architecture finding** (module structure failing to mirror contract structure), never a quiet re-partition (cross-lane dependency ⇒ same lane, or an explicit sequencing gate). One Conductor session per lane, each in its own `.worktrees/<lane>/` with its own `plan/` run-state and lock; the single-session gate reads per-lane. Branch mechanics are unchanged — every unit branch cuts from `origin/dev`, PRs into `dev`, merges on green. A cross-lane merge conflict is a **partition-miss** metric (SPLIT's sibling), counted and sweep-reviewed. c1 defaults lanes=1; lane launcher support is first-prep tooling, built with tests. **The export path stays serial through xor by design**: sweeps are the throughput ceiling because they are the authority act, not a defect; staggered campaign closes are the mitigation.

## 2 · States and the board

`gated → staged → armed → running → (halted ⇄ running) → exhausted → swept → promoted`

**gated** — the pre-cycle state: §0/§1 per-cell gates not yet cleared, or phase-1 prep not begun. A campaign enters **staged** only when phase 1 completes (kickoff + rider + curated backlog drafted, cited-input hashes recorded).

Every transition is recorded on the board (`artifacts/campaigns/board.md`) — one row per member, updated at transitions, never silently. `halted` is re-enterable (xor removes the breaker after the named remedy); `exhausted` is the Conductor's own sticky breaker on an empty backlog — the normal end.

## 3 · The cycle — seven phases, three xor acts

1. **Prepare** *(steward)* — rulings current for this member; machine provisioned (provision script verified against the environment manifest, §11); staging drift-free (`install-configs.sh --check`); segment map current and clause-covering + guidance memos current for the segments in scope (L6, `artifacts/guidance/`); deferred-item binding triggers reviewed (L27); **the ripple run (L24)** when the cited doc hashes changed since the last campaign — doc delta → changed clause ids → the trace (clause → segment → merged units → fixtures) enumerates affected completed work → the test role re-derives adversarial fixtures blind against the NEW clause text and runs them against the existing implementation: green = survival evidenced; red = rework units enter this backlog through the normal plan pipeline — completed work is never silently invalidated and never assumed valid; the ripple also regenerates the compiled context from the amended triad (L27 — derived layers are regenerated, never hand-drifted); **backlog decomposed to intended units for the campaign in scope (L10, rolling-wave)** — per-segment decomposition worksheets authored by a **dispatched, bounded Claude Code plan handoff (L12)** — fresh context; map row + memo + clauses in, candidate units under the sizing rule out, into `campaigns/<id>/` — then attacked by an **adversarial plan-review handoff** per worksheet (write-blocked, findings cite sizing-rule/map/clause + location + severity, uncited ⇒ advisory; fixes ⇒ fresh planner dispatch, cap 3 rounds); the steward dispatches and dispositions; **xor is not routinely involved** — backlog acceptance is implicit at ARM via the kickoff's hash citation, and unresolvable plan findings route to xor through the normal finding path; each backlog entry carries clause set · size class · dependencies, coverage-checked against the segment map; beyond the campaign horizon, map-level only; kickoff + rider drafted into `campaigns/<id>/`; every cited input hashed (§7); hashes recorded in the planning brief (standing).
2. **Arm** *(xor act, through his console — L37/L39)* — at xor's word the steward removes `plan/HALTED.md`, warning first only if the arm-gate shows an issue; the owner record quotes the word. **ARM is legal only on a green arm-gate** (`instance/arm-gate.md`, L13): the steward runs the battery and files the receipt in `campaigns/<id>/` before requesting the act — prevention before detection, detection never softened. Nothing else starts a campaign or resumes a halted one. The arm record is the first entry type of the **owner record**, which generalizes: **every owner act lands a one-line record** (date · act · scope · one-line why) — provenance, never permission; the doctrine binds agents and loops, not xor. Two tiers, scope-routed: `reports/owner-records.md` (family/fleet/machine acts) and `reports/<id>/owner-records.md` (that campaign's lifecycle acts — arm, promote, interventions); ambiguous → family tier, naming the campaign.
3. **Run** *(loop)* — unattended; the Conductor writes only its member repo (code + `plan/`); mid-run correction only by hashed kickoff amendment through the file channel, never chat. **Brief-atomicity (L24):** a dispatched unit completes against its dispatched basis; amendments bind from the next dispatch (the Conductor re-reads the channel between units); an amendment carrying `invalidates:` unit/clause ids ⇒ the named in-flight work is abandoned (recorded, never integrated) and re-plans under the amended basis. The safe mid-run change protocol is the triad: **pause → change (MAYDAY if authoritative) → reconcile → resume**. **L5 governs the lane split:** the Conductor conducts and never composes — content generation rides role-prompted generator invocations; deterministic judges run before the Conductor reads any diff; the session cycles at `units_per_session` (default 10) with the Run-state table as the resume point.
4. **Retrospect** *(steward)* — authored from run artifacts into `campaigns/<id>/retrospective.md` on the standing shape: facts table · the minted DEFAULT surface (family-wide vs member-local per row) · lessons grouped (blockers / consistency / efficiency / quality gaps judges can't see / what worked) · a sweep-input list with proposed routes and stated leans.
5. **Sweep** *(steward drafts · xor rules)* — every minted DEFAULT and every finding receives a disposition, resolved by xor by number. Routes, exactly four:
   - **ratify** → runtime corpus (`artifacts/contracts/`, xor's hand) — runtime content;
   - **rule** → FR register (xor's hand) — family process law;
   - **fix** → the next curated backlog — member-local repair;
   - **defer** → destination + authority + trigger, recorded.
   Runtime content and dev convention never land in the same register — the wall, applied to the sweep. No default survives a campaign undispositioned.
   **Intent trace (§15):** every disposition records its intent field — leans: `followed / diverged-because-X / none-applicable`; rules: `satisfied / halted-on`. Intent entries that survived contact get promoted to basis here (spec content, FR entries) by xor's ratifying act; entries proven wrong are struck (superseded, never erased). **Rule-creep watch:** a register accreting many rules is a spec begging to be authored — flagged, not forbidden.
6. **Absorb** *(xor + steward)* — rulings land in their documents; staging updated; install re-run; `--check` clean before the next campaign prepares.
7. **Promote** *(xor act)* — dev → main review; review findings logged item-by-item into the sweep record (Q2's human column, filled).

**Sovereign signals (L22).** PAN-PAN steers a running campaign (override.env; the run keeps flying). MAYDAY takes positive control — walls off, halt armed — and under a declared MAYDAY **xor's authority is total: anything may change by his hand, including the authoritative docs in `contracts/` and the registers**; no wall, protocol, or doctrine gates it, and no agent records his MAYDAY changes as drift, breach, or finding. MAYDAY offers a Conductor action at declaration — **pause** (freeze mid-flight via the breaker's `OWNER_PAUSE` lane, liveness wires suspended, resumable by `resume` alone; L23), halt (breaker, sticky), or walls-only. Stand-down is `walls on` plus the **MAYDAY reconciliation**: hashes of every changed authority recomputed; any campaign citing a changed doc is re-cited by kickoff amendment or halts for re-prep at its next hash check (mechanical consequence, never a gate on xor); the register records the delta — append-only integrity restored by record even where the emergency edited in place — and where completed work discharged a changed clause, the **ripple (L24)** runs as part of reconciliation.

## 4 · Footing doctrine — standing *(P1)*

- **Spec-bearing members** run under the **standing footing license**: where the cited basis is silent, ambiguous, or self-conflicting **on a matter interior to the member's own cell**, the Conductor resolves it in intent order (§15): an applicable `rule:` **answers** the silence (no mint); an applicable `lean:` means the Conductor **mints along the lean**; otherwise it mints the most conservative fail-closed default — in every minting case recorded as `DEFAULT-<n>` in the append-only findings ledger with its intent trace, and continues. **The license is interior-scoped (§0, FR-011): a silence whose answer would be family-wide or cross-cell is a halt, never a mint** — the conflict routes to xor. Defaults are provisional and never citable as authority; **phase 5 is the license's mandatory closure** — the license is not granted for a campaign whose sweep will not run.
- **The license never covers a known conflict between two ratified texts.** A known conflict is a halt at prepare time (the member does not arm) or at run time (breaker planted, conflict routed to xor). Each loop minting the answer the other repo's contract contradicts is the exact failure the license must not produce.
- **Spec-less members are never licensed.** Plan-only at most; read-only posture enforced from the tool side (Serena `read_only: true`, the ruled strong form) until their specs exist.
- Environment walls, judges, and auth failures halt regardless of the license. The breaker (`plan/HALTED.md`) is sticky; only xor removes it.

## 5 · Roles *(P5)*

- **The Conductor** — runner: **Claude Code**, one session per member run, launcher-started, unattended. Sequences ratified units within their dependencies (**L10 — decomposition is prep's**; the Conductor decomposes only via L9 splits, each a recorded prep-miss); assembles each unit brief (sandwich rule: anchors → task + verbatim clauses → task-local digest); dispatches the **roles (L7)** — per unit: test-writer (brief + verbatim clauses only, **never the diff**; writes `tests/` only, clause-named fixture families) → loop vets tests against the brief (scope check) → implementer (brief + tests by path) → judges → reviewer; every dispatch one bounded `codex exec` invocation, fire-and-return, `--ephemeral`, zero-credential (env-scrubbed at spawn), `--json` stream captured; runs the deterministic judges (**before reading any diff — L5**; post-implement red is **arbitrated**: test-bug / code-bug / spec-ambiguity, the brief as authority; per-role retries `generator_retry_cap` default 3; unit total `unit_invocation_cap` default 5, then `blocked`); runs the reviewer round per §12 (L7 — supersedes finder/advocate, inheriting both duties; findings cite clause + location + severity, uncited → advisory); **L8 record formats:** `VET` line per test-vet · `DIV-<n>` per arbitration in the findings ledger · Run-state table on a fixed schema (columns + phase enum per doctrine) · the test claims' Intended API surface is unit basis at shape level unless clause-contradicting (then a Finding); **L14 outage posture:** a CI-vendor outage is a throughput dip, not a breaker — the Conductor continues locally, defers merges (`ci-deferred`, never merging without CI green), and halts only when no unit is schedulable; deferred PRs re-judge and merge at recovery. **L9 recursion, demoted to pressure valve by L10:** oversized or repeatedly-diverging units still split (fresh caps, scope-narrowing only, depth 3, `SPLIT` record) — but each `SPLIT` is a prep-miss metric, counted per campaign and reviewed at sweep to tune the sizing rule; executes the disposition table on findings (contract-cited → amended-brief redo or `blocked` · operationalizable → fixture minted **via a generator invocation**, then rejudge · advisory → record-only, always in the PR body; **one** audit-triggered redo per unit on green judges); holds `GH_TOKEN` and owns push, PR, and merge into `dev` under green judges — its own reading of a diff certifies nothing. **Conducts, never composes (L5):** briefs point at paths, never paste repo file bodies; exit codes and tails before full logs; session cycle ~10 units (`units_per_session`); per-invocation `role=` recorded (c1 baseline: `code`); the Conductor's model is unchanged for c1 — a measured decision at retro, from the role-split cost data. Writes only `plan/` and git mechanics — **hands off source by wall** (all code and test writes ride generator invocations); never touches `main`; never reads or writes the root. **The pass, recorded:** unit → brief (hashed) → generator invocation → judges + disposition → per-pass record (unit id · doc hashes · brief hash · commit shas · verdicts · findings · usage from `turn.completed` · disposition).
- **The steward** — **xor's interface to the system (L21)**: the only root-scoped session, staffed by Fable, owned by xor; standing acts autonomous per ruled law; owner acts executed through the session at xor's word — the steward warns of foreseeable issues, never seeks approval (L39) — never initiated; hands ungated within the user (L40); repo edits warn-comply-record at xor's word (L42) — the records' truth kept by the warning and the record, never by refusal. Charter is staged material: `agent-configs/root/CLAUDE.md`. Write set: `reports/` · `briefs/` · `campaigns/`. A finder, never a certifier — its "all clear" certifies nothing; its findings are the product.
- **The planner** — a **dispatched station (L12)**: per in-scope segment at Prepare, one bounded Claude Code plan handoff authors the decomposition worksheet, and one adversarial plan-review handoff attacks it; the steward dispatches both and executes disposition — the Conductor only sequences. Contract and experience-spec authoring stays design-session work; drafts still land as proposals in `reports/proposals/` per root rule 5.
- **xor** — the recorded acts, literal hand, always: **arm** (breaker removal) · **rule** (pins, FR entries) · **ratify** (`contracts/`, registers) · **promote** (campaign int → main; D40). The steward prepares each act down to a one-line confirmation and performs none.

## 6 · Run-state custody *(P6)*

- `plan/` in each member repo is the Conductor's writable run-state surface: findings ledger, backlog with its Run-state table (the resume entry point), unit briefs, claims, breaker.
- It is **never committed**: **excluded run state** — the *exclusion* is planted (owned by `install-configs.sh`); the content is loop-written, never staged — excluded via `.git/info/exclude`. **The exclusion is owned by `install-configs.sh`** — its managed EXCLUDES block is the one component that already legally writes this class (the Conductor is walled off `.git/`, the steward has no hands); the staged EXCLUDES gains `plan/` and replants. **`git clean` is banned in the Conductor protocol** — excluded state is exposed to it (belt to the member AGENTS.md braces, which already forbid it).
- **Breaker durability:** excluded run state never leaves the machine on its own — so **on any halt detection, the custody sweep fires as the steward's first act**, before any other deliverable; the breaker and ledger tail reach `reports/` custody before diagnosis is even reported.
- Custody: the steward sweeps `plan/` into `artifacts/reports/<id>/` (append-only) at every halt and at campaign close. **The sweep is scripted and emits a hash manifest** — byte-verifiable custody, never bare copies. Which corpus and which config governed pass N is answered by recorded hashes, never by memory.

## 7 · Inputs and citation discipline

**IDs are citations.** Every decision/clause ID series registers in `registers/id-namespaces.md` before its first use; IDs travel in **qualified form** (pin P16 · guarantee V4 · harvest Q3 · <member>-c<N> DEFAULT-003); **a bare number binds nothing** — a malformed citation is fixed, never interpreted. Ledger-scoped series (DEFAULT-·, F-PLAN/F-RUN-·) carry their campaign qualifier whenever they leave their ledger.

A kickoff cites **every** input by path + content hash: the registers, the member's deliverable-doc set per the standard (L26: lexicon · contracts · experience spec + machine exports, cited by docs-manifest hash), **the intent register (family file + member file where present — advisory class, binding per force level, consultation mandatory)**, the shared assets — dependency ledger · environment manifest · segment map + per-segment guidance (advisory force, L6, `artifacts/guidance/`) · finder checklist · fixture corpus — and the brief template. A cited text is basis; **silence within a cited text is license territory (§4)**; an uncited text binds nothing. Hash mismatch at pass time is a halt. Family law travels by citation, never by copy.

## 8 · Landing protocol *(P10/P11)*

Authorship (design sessions) · authority (xor) · execution (the root session in scribe mode) stay separate. Ratified text lands on the machine by **landing prompt**: verbatim bytes to stated paths, per-file sha256, write → hash → compare → one transcription record appended to `reports/` (path · source hash · match · ordered-by) → one report line per file. **Mismatch or gap halts the landing; the scribe never fills blanks** — improvisation is authorship, and authorship does not live on that side. Config never lands where it runs: staging → xor runs install → session restart (Etherel's law). Fallback on a mangled paste: the batch ships as a zip, same hashes — never unverified either way. **Bootstrap was manual, xor's hand, one-time — recorded here as the exception.** Every landing closes with `smoke.sh` (deterministic, read-only self-check; `--landing` mode adds the batch hash manifest) — all PASS/SKIP or the landing isn't done.

## 9 · Campaign files *(P3)*

- `artifacts/campaigns/board.md` — the fleet surface; the only place campaign state is authoritative.
- `artifacts/campaigns/<id>/` — kickoff (+ per-campaign rider), curated backlog, retrospective, sweep. Kickoffs live here; the interim `prompts/` holds only operator prompts until the shared kickoff base (a named c2 prep artifact) lands.
- **The kickoff-base rewrite is a named c2 prep artifact**: run-state updates never ride merges (§6 — excluded state can't), and the footing license is withdrawn for matters already ruled.
- The shared kickoff base and the per-campaign rider are split so family law arrives by citation (§7), never by copy-drift.

## 10 · Judges — the standing set *(I1–I4)*

Deterministic, file-resident, per-PR, every member. Fixture families are founded from the target spec's MUST / MUST NOT clauses at campaign founding — and per unit, the adversarial fixtures are the test role's product, authored blind to the implementation (L7); the universal property — **refusal ⇒ exit 1 ⇒ nothing changed** — is the first family, snapshot-compared across every verb.

- The four cargo judges: `check` · `test` · `clippy -D warnings` · `fmt --check`.
- **Vocabulary judge** *(I1)* — banned/reserved-term lint over identifiers, docs, comments, and commit messages; term list from the member's `term-export.txt` once its docs ratify.
- **Join-key judge** *(I2)* — every commit's `Unit:/Pass:/Brief:/Config:` trailers present, well-formed, and the brief hash resolving to a real file. Protects the record↔commit join.

Conditional, activating with their prerequisites:

- **Fixture-corpus judge** *(I3)* — runs a member's parser against the language-neutral TOML corpus (`instance/fixtures/`); activates when the corpus exports.
- **Failure-vocabulary judge** *(I4)* — refusal ⇒ 1 · halt ⇒ 2 · fault ⇒ 3 · success ⇒ 0, nothing-changed snapshot-compared; standard for binary members, per each member's binding.


## 11 · Preflight and provisioning *(I5, I10; job description per §0)*

**Provisioning's job is to make the machine honor containment.** The known leaks are the harness violating the architecture — shared `~/.rustup`/`~/.cargo` homes (the shared-home rustup race class), the single codex trust ledger, credential-store lock contention — and provisioning's acceptance test is their repair: **Conductors never write shared state at run time** (the pinned toolchain pre-installed and immutable, registries pre-warmed), **trust pre-recorded fleet-wide**, **contention-free credential paths**. A provisioned machine is one on which concurrent cells cannot touch each other.

- **Environment manifest** — one ruled file (`instance/assets/environment-manifest.md`): every tool a member run needs — toolchain channel/profile/components, system packages, CLIs — with a verify command per entry. Sibling to the dependency ledger, never merged with it: crates are the Conductor's inputs, tools are the machine's. **The manifest pins the serena version** (D36); wall (3) extends to assert it once the manifest is authored — the version-resolution command shape is a first-run verify item. Provisioning's serena leg also sets `trusted_project_path_patterns` (`repos/*` + `.worktrees/*`) — on serena ≥1.6 a fresh home trusts nothing and planted project settings silently stop applying; `serena-fleet.sh` warns loud when the key is absent.
- **Provision step** — `provision.sh` at the root installs *from* the manifest; run once per machine before any campaign, re-run only when the manifest changes; includes machine git posture (the repo-local `credential.useHttpPath=false` fix, re-applied on every fresh clone). **No campaign arms on an unprovisioned machine.**
- **Preflight** — one script, run before every counted pass: env walls (mounts, toolchain, gh auth, push-token verify), cited-input hash verification (§7 mechanized), staging `--check`, then the uncounted fmt+clippy warmup (bastel analog: shellcheck/shfmt). Preflight verifies against the same manifest provisioning installed from — one source, checkable. A red preflight costs seconds, not a pass.
- **Four known walls, in the preflight list by name:** (1) **codex repo trust pre-recorded** for the target member — `codex exec` never prompts and never writes trust; an untrusted repo **silently drops its project config** (sandbox posture, permissions, Serena) and runs on defaults; (2) **single-live-session gate** — root rule 8, mechanically checked; (3) **serena health** — the planted MCP line assumes a working `serena` CLI and fleet context; (4) **model + effort resolution asserted against the manifest** — the manifest pins `model`, `model_reasoning_effort`, and `model_reasoning_summary` (FR-010); a fresh machine silently runs defaults.
- **Upgrade re-verification** *(D38)* — any upgrade of the runner (`claude`) or the generator (`codex`) re-runs the smoke campaign to green before the next arm. Walls are version-validated facts, never standing truths; the smoke campaign is the standing re-validation instrument.
- **Repo-side D25 state splits in two.** Default branch is steward-readable (`gh repo view`) and reportable as fact. **Protections and required-check state** need an admin-capable credential the steward doesn't hold — that verification is **xor's provisioning act, tracked on the board's fleet row**; preflight and `/prep` assert CI workflow files present and default-branch facts, never server-side requiredness.

## 12 · The reviewer *(I8, I9, FR-013 — L7: one consolidated adversarial round, superseding the separate finder and advocate)*

- **An adversary with fresh context, walls of its own.** **Mechanism:** a dedicated, bounded `codex exec` invocation dispatched by the Conductor — fire-and-return like the generator's, but **read-only sandbox**: a finder that structurally cannot mutate. Fresh context by construction (a fresh invocation, not a sibling thread), and — unlike a spawned sub-agent — its permissions genuinely differ from the generator's. Inputs ride its brief (the unit diff, claims, cited clauses); findings land file-resident. Its brief is to break the unit: attack the claims file, hunt green-but-wrong implementations, probe the residue §10's lints don't mechanize — cross-product gaps, normalization edges, precedence ratchets, docs claiming more than checks prove.
- **Stochastic posture, bounded authority.** It samples attack angles; **its silence certifies nothing** — a finder, never a certifier. Anything it raises is a recorded finding in the ledger, own class, brief hash recorded (the record captures what was raised; the pass is not replayable).
- **Cadence (L7):** one batched round per unit, **on the green diff after judges**. Findings absorb as fixes via a fresh implementer dispatch (within `unit_invocation_cap`); anything unresolved or out-of-scope routes to the sweep. **Findings contract:** clause (or intent entry) + location + severity per finding; uncited findings auto-route advisory. Findings never block a merge mechanically.
- **Mechanize what can be** *(I9)*: the wildcard-match ratchet via lint configuration, the banned act-word grep for types-only docs — moved from finder brief to judges as they prove out, shrinking the finder's load to the genuinely judgmental.
- **The advocate — xor's representative inside the Conductor** *(FR-013)*. Second dedicated read-only invocation, same mechanism as the finder; brief inputs: the intent register (§15), the experience spec's judgment register, the member spec. It reads the unit asking *is this what xor would want* and raises findings only: `rule:` unsatisfied (routes through the normal finding→halt path — never its own authority), `lean:` divergence without a recorded reason, taste drift the judges can't see, and **intent gaps** ("the register is silent here and xor probably has an opinion" — the map of what intent to write next, generated in-run). One batched round per unit, adjacent to or fused with the finder round (FR-007 cost data decides fusion). **It is not xor**: it never resolves pins, never blesses, never mints intent; silence certifies nothing; its misreads are sweep data that tune the register. Altitude split, one line: the steward represents xor *above* the Conductor; the advocate, *inside* it; neither has hands.

## 13 · Instrumentation and assembly *(I6, I7, I12)*

- **Per-pass record, mandatory, loop-side** *(I6)*: wall-clock timestamps plus token usage from the generator's `turn.completed` JSONL event, appended to the run record (`plan/`, custody-swept to `reports/`) — cost per pass is mechanized, not estimated.
- **Trace custody — generator-side only** *(I12, FR-010)*: every `codex exec` invocation (generator, finder, advocate) runs `--ephemeral` — no vendor rollouts exist. The Conductor captures each invocation's `--json` JSONL stream at write time into `plan/` (its writable surface); the custody sweep carries them to `reports/<id>/traces/`, hash-manifested — capture at the source, custody at the sweep. **Fidelity label mandatory:** these carry vendor reasoning *summaries*, never raw chain-of-thought — telemetry, never authority, never citable as basis, never a steering channel. The manifest pins `model_reasoning_summary`. Loop-runner (Claude Code) stream custody — **amended, D37**: the launcher tees the Conductor's full `stream-json` to `reports/<id>/loop-<ts>.jsonl` (session id from the init event; per-turn joins from second zero). Same fidelity walls as every trace: reasoning summaries at most — telemetry, never authority, never citable as basis, never a steering channel.
- **Adherence-decay measurement** over real sessions becomes possible against this trace custody; the measurement method itself is an open design item.
- **Comprehension probe** (`probe.sh` + `instance/tools/probe-questions.md`/`probe-key.md`): fixed scenarios, closed enums, exact-match grading — model inference isn't deterministic, the *verdict* is. - **PR bodies are generated, never hand-written** *(I7)*: claims file + appended CI-evidence block (`instance/tools/`), making the two surfaces one.

## 14 · Shared assets and the crib *(I10)*

Homes: `instance/assets/` — dependency ledger · environment manifest · engineering crib · finder checklist; `instance/fixtures/` — the language-neutral corpus. All kickoff-cited per §7.

## 15 · The intent register *(the framework for xor's intent — enable and guide)*

A third authority class beside basis (binding) and evidence (record): **intent — advisory, force-leveled, xor's voice.** Files: `artifacts/intent/family.md` + `intent/<member>.md` as needed; append-only, plain speech, entries in seconds not sessions. **xor edits these live — the one `artifacts/` exception to the landing protocol** `[drafting decision — confirm: C7]` — friction kills the mechanism; staleness never does (append-only + citation hashes at kickoff keep every run's snapshot recorded).

- **Force levels.** `lean:` guides — licensed silence resolves along it; divergence legal with recorded reason. `rule:` gates — micro-basis without the ceremony: no divergence path, conflicts halt, a silence it answers is answered.
- **Precedence, one line:** ratified basis > `rule:` > `lean:` > conservative default. A `rule:` conflicting with ratified basis loses and surfaces as a finding — the register never silently forks the law.
- **Teeth.** (1) **Consultation is mandatory**: intent files are cited kickoff inputs (§7) and named steward inputs; resolving an applicable silence without checking intent — or against it, unrecorded — is a finding; the advocate (§12) is the independent check. (2) **Non-blocking by construction** at the mechanism level: absence of intent never halts; only entries xor marks `rule:` gate — xor choosing to block, in a thirty-second channel. (3) **Traceability**: every mint and steward lean carries its intent field (§3.5). (4) **Promotion**: surviving entries become basis at sweeps; the register stays small because it keeps graduating.
- **The landing channel.** Entries drafted in design sessions land through **checked proposals** — verified at the authoritative layer (live disk), never at the design session's lagging mirror: duplicate/supersede → basis-collision (a colliding `rule:` is a refusal) → force-level sanity; refuse-on-trip lands nothing. Both legs are files in `xor/channel/` (inbox proposals · outbox echoes); the echo's sha256 is the design session's sync token. Protocol + templates: `instance/tools/intent-landing-template.md`. xor's paste authorizes forward; xor's ask-gate click authorizes the echo write.
- **Not register bleed (Q6):** this steers *through* a document — written, hashed, cited — the file-only correction channel generalized from corrections to guidance.
