# Where things are

Status is the column that matters, and it is **standing**, not age:
**authority** to obey, **method** to learn from, **record** as evidence of one
past run. Nothing here is filed by how old it is.

## The kernel

| path | status | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `loopstrap_core/` | **live** | 27 modules. Authority, workflow, contracts, evidence ledger, recursive driver, harness execution, recovery, budget, telemetry mirror. Zero third-party imports. |
| `spec/cue/` | **live** | The production schemas: project package, Cell and composite contracts, evidence, acceptance. Half of every two-producer check. |
| `config/*.v1.json` | **live** | Versioned data the kernel is driven by. Roles, Role-Treatments, harness profiles, workflow, tool pins, seal rules, the gate budget. |

## Two things that are not where you would guess

**The driver has no operator entry point.** `LoopDriver` is constructed in
exactly three places, all in `tests/readiness/test_driver.py`, and all three
inject a fake role-result callable. The CLI has five subcommands and every one is
a validator or a replay. There is no `run`.

**The gate is full.** Six invariants against a cap of six in
`config/gate-budget.v1.json`. Adding one costs a deletion or a deliberate cap
raise, and the cap is sealed, so raising it shows in the seal delta.
